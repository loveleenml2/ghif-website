About GHIF

A movement of hope, resilience and practical transformation

Founded in 2023, the Genuine Happiness International Foundation – Uganda Chapter serves youth and women who have been excluded from formal education. We believe self-development is the foundation of change: through vocational training, skills-building and mentorship, GHIF restores power — the power to take control and transform lives.

By equipping young women and men with real-world, job-ready skills, we open doors to employment, entrepreneurship and dignified livelihoods that create lasting community change across Uganda's under-served regions.

Our project aims

1.Provide inclusive vocational training to underprivileged youth, especially young women and men, enabling independent, dignified livelihoods.
2.Equip adolescents and young adults excluded from formal education with practical, job-ready skills.
3.Foster self-confidence and personal agency through hands-on mentorship and supportive training environments.

Our founder's story: from overlooked to empowered

Loveleen Ayebare Muhoozi — Founding President

"Skills gave me what the system took away—confidence, respect and a reason to lead."

I was not meant to be the founder of anything. I was meant to be forgotten. Growing up with only an O-level education, I lived on the margins — not just economically, but emotionally and socially. I watched my voice disappear while university graduates were celebrated, and slowly I began to believe that without a degree, I had no value, no place, no future.

It wasn't a lack of ambition. It was the absence of access. That painful truth didn't break me — it shaped me. As an adult I returned to education and earned a Bachelor's degree in Political Science and a Master's in Digital Media Management from Birkbeck, University of London — acts of healing, dignity and quiet defiance.

My journey led me to found GHIF for young people pushed out of the formal education system due to poverty, teenage pregnancy, domestic violence and stigma — a platform I wish I had — where worth is not measured by academic credentials but by spirit, skill and lived wisdom.

Every time a graduate shakes my hand, starts a business, or mentors another learner, I am reminded that pain can be turned into purpose, exclusion can birth empowerment, and invisible girls and boys can become visible leaders. This is the heart of GHIF — a journey we're building, together.
